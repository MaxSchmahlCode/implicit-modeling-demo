Why Implicit Modeling?

Implicit modeling represents geometry through mathematical functions, eliminating topology constraints that plague traditional CAD systems. This functional approach enables real-time modifications, guarantees watertight geometry, and naturally supports features for Additive Manufacturing.

Robust Boolean Operations

Constructive Solid Geometry (CSG) of Implicit Functions guarantee robust, watertight models that never fail.

Smooth Blending between Solids

Smooth union operations create organic, tangent-continuous blends between shapes.

Simple Parametric Modeling

Guaranteed to produce valid results through simple arithmetic on distance values.
